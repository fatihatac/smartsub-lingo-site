Learn languages from the shows you already watch

SmartSub Lingo is a browser extension for Netflix, YouTube, HBO Max and MUBI. Hover over any word in the subtitles to see its translation and meanings, hear the pronunciation, and save it to your own dictionary.

Get it on the Chrome Web Store

Features

  • Word translations with multiple meanings, on hover or click
  • Press T to translate the whole subtitle line
  • Smart pause while you read
  • Personal dictionary with CSV / JSON export
  • Choose your translation provider
